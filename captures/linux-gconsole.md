# Scenario: linux-gconsole

- Date: 2026-10-13T07:04Z
- Server: none. The peer is `kdx-probe --listen`, playing GConsole (`%HGC`
  0x40) on 127.0.0.1:10900 in the lab namespace.
- Client: linux 1.620 beta 5
- Server state: not applicable; the peer accepts `admin` with
  `lab-only-pw!1`.

Decode it with `kdx-dissect captures/linux-gconsole.pcapng --password
'lab-only-pw!1'`.

## Steps

The capture follows `.scratch/capture-gaps/issues/11-gconsole.md`. An agent
drove the client with `xdotool`; the GConsole window's content starts at
(6, 22) in its X window, so a click at X (106, 72) lands at (100, 50).

1. Started the peer with one 4000 and one 4001, generated by a script that
   packs a record of each kind:

   ```sh
   lab/kdx-lab run target/debug/kdx-probe --listen 127.0.0.1:10900 \
     --password 'lab-only-pw!1' --table <Client 1.620 beta 5 KDXClient.lexe> \
     --wait 1000 4000:<hex> 4001:<hex>
   ```

2. `kdx-lab client linux`. Connect, Type GConsole, Address `127.0.0.1`,
   login `admin`, password `lab-only-pw!1`, Enter. Hellos at 4 and 6,
   proofs at 8 and 9, 4000 at 10, 4001 at 14. The release of Enter reached
   the new window as a 4200 at 12. The window, "GConsole:
   127.0.0.1:10900", showed every record as the peer meant it.
3. The operator pressed Alt+Print Screen to take a screenshot of the
   window: Alt's press at 16, then a release with no usage and Alt held
   at 18. Like B in Ctrl+B (step 5), Print Screen sent no press; 18 is
   likely its release, and Alt's own release never arrived [I].
4. Left clicks at (100, 50) and (150, 100) (20–26); a left drag from
   (200, 150) to (300, 200) in five moves (28, 30); a right click and a
   middle click at (100, 200) (32–38); one wheel step up (40, 42) and one
   down (44, 46).
5. Keys: `a` (48, 50), Shift+A (52–58), Return (60, 62), Left (64, 66).
   F1 then opened the client's window menu, which took the focus, so F1,
   Ctrl+B, Alt+C, and the Escape that closed the menu sent nothing.
   Ctrl+B again (68–72), BackSpace (74, 76), then `z` held for 1.5 s
   (78–154).
6. Closed the window with its close box: the client closed the
   connection at 156 without sending a message.

## Observations

- The handshake and session decode with GConsole's header scramble and the
  account key of `lab-only-pw!1`. The client sent `%KDX` 0x10 with login
  `admin` (4), and its proof matched the peer's hello (8).
- 4000 (10): width 0x200, height 0x180, colour 0x00336699. The window
  opened at that size, filled with that colour.
- 4001 (14): 21 records, 456 bytes. All drew as the table in
  `docs/protocol/gconsole.md` describes.
- 4201 is the mouse and 4200 the keyboard, the reverse of the guess from
  the event kinds. Each is 16 bytes; mouse moves send nothing.
- 4201 x and y are in window content coordinates (20: (100, 50)). Right
  button down sets 0x00040000 at 0x04 (32). Each wheel step sends two
  identical messages with a delta of +0x78 or −0x78 (40–46).
- 4200 sends the key's USB HID usage and character on press only; releases
  carry 0, except Shift's at 56. Modifiers are as held before the event:
  Shift+A's A is 8 (54), and a release with Alt held is 2 (18).
- Ctrl+B sent Ctrl down (68), then two releases: one with modifiers 0x21
  (70) and one with 0 (72). No press for B arrived.
- Held `z`: the first repeat came 661 ms after the press (78, 80), then a
  release and a press together every 40 ms. Kind 3 never appeared.
